FAQ

Questions you might have.

Does HyCross Care provide diagnosis or treatment directly?

No. We coordinate care — organizing records, matching you with the right U.S. hospital and specialist, and managing logistics. All diagnosis and treatment is provided by licensed physicians at partner institutions.

How does the U.S. medical visa process work?

A formal diagnosis and a hospital acceptance letter make the B-2 medical visa case straightforward for most applicants. We help prepare the documentation your case will need.

What does treatment in the U.S. typically cost?

U.S. specialty care costs vary widely by diagnosis, hospital, and treatment plan. Most leading centers require a deposit before scheduling — your coordinator will walk you through a real, itemized estimate for your specific case.

What if I don't speak English?

We provide interpreter support in English, Russian, and French, accompanying you to appointments and follow-up conversations throughout your treatment.

I'm not in the CIS, Caucasus, or Africa — can I still use this service?

Yes. These regions are our core focus, but we also welcome inquiries from Europe, English-speaking countries, and elsewhere.
